Data and storage

Terra stores your selected language and acknowledgement of the storage notice in your browser's local storage. The project does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies.

External services

The interface loads fonts and libraries from Google Fonts, esm.sh and unpkg, and map imagery from Esri. Your browser connects to these providers when those resources are requested; they may receive your IP address and standard request information.

Area coordinates and requested dates are sent to the Terra API for analysis. The API retrieves imagery and models from external services including AWS Earth Search, the Sentinel archive and Hugging Face. Public deployment is hosted on Vercel. Those providers may process technical request data under their own policies.
